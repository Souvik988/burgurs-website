# Asset attribution

## Restaurant and reference material

Dre's Burgers names, branding, business information, menu descriptions and business/menu photographs relate to the restaurant in Beck Bagan, Kolkata. Sources include its [Zomato listing](https://www.zomato.com/kolkata/dres-burgers-park-circus-area), public [@d.r.es business posts](https://www.instagram.com/d.r.es/) and the Wanderlog business gallery. The supplied [CRAV reference](https://www.cravburgers.shop/) informed the visual direction.

These business and reference assets are not represented as public-domain or openly licensed material. No general open-source license is granted to the restaurant branding, photographs or reference material by this repository. Original-post links and photo credits are retained in the website.

## Generated artwork

Campaign portraits show fictional diners and are labelled as AI-created scenes; they are not customer testimonials. Founder work scenes labelled as creative reconstructions use public kitchen photographs as references. Genuine business photographs and original-host videos are identified separately. Illustrations and image edits were generated with imagegen.

## Fonts

Mouse Memoirs and Modak are self-hosted WOFF2 fonts from Google Fonts, distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Their notices are included alongside the fonts in `public/assets/fonts/`.

## External players and map

Videos use original Instagram, Facebook or YouTube players with source links. The original hosts control availability and may require sign-in. The visit map loads Google Maps when activated. These services require an internet connection.
